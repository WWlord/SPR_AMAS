# SPR_AMAS

We live in the age of mobility. We move through space and time. We move in teams. But what is there for teamwork, and for you as a team member? Teamwork services range from simple email to enterprise-class servers.
Today, talking about simple employee interaction using a mail client is unethical. Of course, a manager will choose an enterprise-class tool, something like ECM or ERP. But where is the mobility in that? An office server or a server hidden in the cloud doesn't offer sufficient mobility. A mobile team is one where everyone, including the manager, moves around with a tablet or laptop slung over their shoulder. They walk into a bar somewhere in Barcelona or Shanghai and communicate with their team, each member of which is located in a random corner of the world. Communication is everywhere. And there's no need to anchor to some data center in the mountains of Sweden. In these challenging times, any member of your team can be cut off from access to the data center for political or legal reasons. And all your mobility goes to hell. We live in difficult times, a time of instability. And only moving from problematic areas of planet Earth to areas with stable legal, political, and economic systems gives your business an advantage. All you need is a server on your laptop, a train or boat ticket, and free internet access.
SPR AMAS will make your business truly free for you and your team.
